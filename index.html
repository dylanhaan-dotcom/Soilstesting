Sample Submission

Follow these steps to submit samples for testing. Questions? Call us before shipping.

01

Contact Us First

Call or email to confirm test requirements, turnaround time, and sample quantities before collecting samples in the field.

02

Collect & Package Samples

Use sealed plastic bags for soils (min. 25 lb for compaction), sealed buckets for concrete, and secure containers for cores. Label each sample with project name, location, date, and depth/lift.

03

Complete Chain of Custody

Fill out and include a chain-of-custody (COC) form with each shipment. List tests requested, project number, engineer of record, and any special instructions.

04

Drop Off or Ship

Deliver samples to our lab during business hours, or ship via carrier. Concrete cylinders must be transported within 30 minutes of casting (ASTM C31). Call ahead for large shipments.

05

Receive Results

Test reports are delivered via email PDF. Standard turnaround is 5–7 business days; rush (48-hour) service is available for most tests at an additional fee.

About the Lab

We are an independent materials testing laboratory serving contractors, engineers, municipalities, and developers. Our technicians are trained to current ASTM and AASHTO standards, and our equipment is regularly calibrated to maintain measurement accuracy.

Whether you need routine field compaction verification, a full geotechnical investigation support package, or QC/QA testing on a state DOT project, we provide accurate, defensible results on time.
